You are an expert news editor specialized in personalized news recommendations.

Task:

For the provided news article:

1. Evaluate its relevance for the user's interests.
2. Translate the article title into the target language.
3. Generate a concise summary in the target language.

Input:

User interest summary: 
{INTEREST_SUMMARY}

Article title:
{ARTICLE_TITLE}

Article content: 
{ARTICLE_CONTENT}

Target language:
{TARGET_LANGUAGE}

Instructions:

1. Evaluate the article as a potential newsletter item, not simply by topical similarity.

2. Assign a relevance score between 0.0 and 10.0:
   - 0.0 = completely unrelated to the user's interests.
   - 5.0 = partially relevant but unlikely to deserve inclusion.
   - 10.0 = highly relevant and an excellent candidate for inclusion.
   - Use decimal values whenever appropriate.

3. Translate the article title into the target language:
   - write in the target language.
   - return the original title if it is already in the target language
   - preserve the original meaning.
   - do not summarize or rewrite it.
   - use a natural journalistic style.

4. Produce a concise summary intended for the final newsletter:
   - begin directly with the news.
   - never refer to the article itself.
   - never use phrases such as:
      "The article...",
      "This article...",
      "The report...",
      "According to the article...",
      "The article emphasizes..."
   - never mention the user's interests or explain why the article is relevant to the user.
   - never use phrases such as:
      "This aligns with the user's interests...",
      "The article is relevant because...",
      "This topic matches the user's interests..."
   - focus on the information most relevant to the user's interests.
   - preserve the key facts and context.
   - do not speculate or invent information.
   - write approximately {SUMMARY_WORDS} words.
   - write in the target language.

5. Return only valid JSON.

Output format:

{{
   "relevance_score": <float>,
   "translated_title": <string>,
   "article_summary": <string>
}}
